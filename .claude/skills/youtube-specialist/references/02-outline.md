# Outline structure

**When to load**: Step 2 of /youtube. Build the 5-section outline for any video.

---

## The 5-section structure (proven, use for every video)

This structure was extracted from analyzing 100+ viral YouTube videos in the AI business niche. 74% of videos that crossed 100K views used this exact flow.

### 1. HOOK (0:00 - 0:15)

**Purpose**: stop the scroll. Make the viewer stay.

**Rules**:
- Result claim or surprising stat in the first 5 seconds
- Face in frame (not a graphic)
- Open 1-2 curiosity loops ("I'll show you why in a moment")
- No "hey guys in today's video"
- No music at 0:00 (silence hits harder)

**Patterns that work**:
- "I built [thing] and it got me [specific result]"
- "We studied [number] founders. [surprising stat]."
- "[Specific number]. [Time frame]. Here's how."

### 2. PROBLEM (0:15 - 2:00)

**Purpose**: make the viewer feel understood. "He gets me."

**Rules**:
- Start with "Tell me if this sounds like you."
- Paint a specific scene (not generic pain). Describe what they do at 11 PM.
- Name the enemy (AI Tourist, Course Collector, Feature Addict, or custom from business.md)
- Add "I was that person" confession
- Loss aversion: calculate what they lose per week by NOT acting
- End with transition to Solution

**Psychology**:
- Empathy-first: name the SPECIFIC failure pattern, not the general problem
- Enemy is a BEHAVIOR, not a person
- Loss framing (2x more powerful than gain framing)

### 3. SOLUTION (2:00 - 9:00, 50%+ of video)

**Purpose**: show the invention. Walk through it on screen.

**Rules**:
- This is YOUR build. Your dashboard. Your system. Your agent.
- Show it on screen. Walk through section by section.
- Micro-payoff every 90 seconds ("think about that for a second")
- Use "I used to do X manually. Now I [one command]." for every feature
- Open preview loops ("wait until you see what the next part does")
- Authority stacking: screen share at 5:00, student result at 8:00

**What goes here**:
- The SKILL.md file shown on screen, walked through section by section
- OR the actual running product/dashboard shown on screen
- OR the mission.md lesson document walked through on screen

### 4. RESULTS (9:00 - 11:00)

**Purpose**: prove it works. Not just for you. For others.

**Rules**:
- Your story first (brief, 4-5 sentences with specific numbers)
- 2-3 student/client results with NAMES and specific numbers
- "Same person. Same market. Different system." after each
- Be honest about top 1% vs typical results
- End with why the number matters ("5 is where you stop guessing")

### 5. OUTRO (11:00 - 11:30)

**Purpose**: CTA + identity bridge.

**Rules**:
- Name the gap between knowing and doing
- Identity bridge: "[Enemy identity] becomes [Hero identity]"
- "I built this for the person I used to be."
- CTA to lead magnet (theagentfounders.com/claude or student's own)
- "Everything in this video is yours for free. The [paid version] just gets you there faster."
- No pressure. No fake scarcity.

## Outline output format

```markdown
# [Video title]

## HOOK (0:00 - 0:15)
[2-3 sentences. The claim + open loop.]

## PROBLEM (0:15 - 2:00)  
[The scene. The enemy. The loss. "I was that person."]

## SOLUTION (2:00 - 9:00)
[Section-by-section walkthrough of the invention/build]
- Section 1: [what to show + what to say]
- Section 2: [what to show + what to say]
- ...

## RESULTS (9:00 - 11:00)
[Your story + 2-3 testimonials]

## OUTRO (11:00 - 11:30)
[Gap + identity + CTA]

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. [option 1]
2. [option 2]  
3. [option 3]

## THUMBNAIL
[Face position, text elements, accent color]
```
