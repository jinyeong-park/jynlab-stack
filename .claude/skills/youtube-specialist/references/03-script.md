# Script writing rules

**When to load**: Step 3 of /youtube. Write the teleprompter script from an outline.

---

## Format

Pure teleprompter. Every word in the script is the word Chris (or the student) reads into the camera. No scene directions. No camera notes. No B-roll cues. Those go in a separate production guide for the video editor, not in the script.

## Target length

1,800 to 2,500 spoken words. At 150 wpm = 12-17 minutes raw. Edits down to 10-15 minutes final.

## Voice rules

1. **No em dashes.** Use periods, commas, or line breaks.
2. **No Tier 1/2 banned words**: delve, leverage, foster, harness, encompass, landscape, tapestry, robust, testament, pivotal, underscore, realm, embark, interplay, nuanced, garner, showcase, navigate, facilitate, optimize, elevate, unlock, streamline, innovative, revolutionary, transformative, holistic, comprehensive, cutting-edge, groundbreaking, seamless.
3. **Sentence length varies**: short, then long, then short. No uniform rhythm.
4. **First person**: "I built this. I ran this. Here is what happened."
5. **Specific numbers always**: if you catch yourself writing "a lot," stop and put the real number.
6. **Tribal vocabulary**: AI Tourist, Course Collector, Feature Addict, Agent Founder, Seller, Operator, Director, Founder, The Scoreboard, The Flywheel, Cash block, "Shut up and execute."

## Psychology techniques (apply in order)

### Hook (first 30 seconds)
- Open 2-3 curiosity loops before the 30-second mark
- Result or stat in the first line
- "I'll show you why in a moment" / "I'll tell you that story at the end"

### Problem
- "Tell me if this sounds like you" opener
- Paint the specific scene (what they do at 11 PM, what Thursday feels like)
- Name the enemy identity ("I call this the [Enemy]. I was that person.")
- Loss aversion: "$X per week you are losing. Not missing. Losing."
- "I lived in that gap for [time]"

### Solution
- "Think about that for a second" after every major reveal (every 90 seconds)
- "I used to do X. Now I do Y." for the strongest features
- "Why did I build it this way? Because [personal experience]."
- Preview loops: "Wait until you see the next part"

### Results
- Authority stacking: own number first, then student results
- "Same person. Different system." after each testimonial
- Honest framing: "The $X is top 1%. Most founders will be [realistic names]."

### Outro
- Name the gap: "You now know the framework. The question is 3 months alone or 3 weeks with..."
- Identity bridge: "[Enemy identity] becomes [Hero identity]"
- "I built this for the person I used to be."
- "Everything in this video is yours for free."

## Reading the business.md

Before writing, read business.md for:
- Niche (who the video helps)
- Enemy (the identity to name in Problem)
- Voice (how the student actually talks)
- Offer (what to reference in Solution)
- Proof/testimonials (for Results section)

## What NOT to include in the script

- Scene directions ("cut to screen recording here")
- B-roll cues ("show screenshot of dashboard")
- Music notes ("BG music enters at 0:18")
- Camera instructions ("medium close, direct to lens")

All of those go in a separate PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md for the video editor. The script is pure spoken words.
