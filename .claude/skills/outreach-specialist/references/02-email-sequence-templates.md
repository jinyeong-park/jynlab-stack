## Sequence rules (apply to all niches)

1. **Under 100 words per email.** No exceptions. Count them.
2. **Subject lines under 6 words.** No ALL CAPS. No "!!!"
3. **No em dashes.** Use periods, commas, or line breaks.
4. **No Tier 1/2 banned words.** No "leverage," "comprehensive," "innovative," "game-changer."
5. **Personalization**: {{firstName}} in subject (Step 1, 3, 4) and body. {{companyName}} optional.
6. **Tone**: casual, short, reads like a real person wrote it at 7 AM before coffee.
7. **No "I hope this email finds you well."** No "just wanted to reach out." No "quick question for you."
8. **Step 1 always has A/B test.** Two subject line variants. Split 50/50.
9. **Step 5 is always a breakup.** No pressure. Free resource. YouTube link. "Good luck building."
10. **Every email has ONE call to action.** Not two. Not three. One link, one ask.

## How students customize for their niche

1. Read business.md for: niche, enemy, offer, pain point, voice
2. Use Chris's sequence as the structure template (5 steps, same day schedule)
3. Replace the pain point with the student's niche-specific pain from business.md
4. Replace the offer with the student's deliverable
5. Replace the CTA link with the student's landing page or lead magnet
6. Run humanizer-check on every email before saving to cold_campaign_config
7. Always A/B test Step 1
